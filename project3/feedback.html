Use this form to describe a request, backlog problem, refinement issue, or prioritization question. The goal is to collect enough context to understand the problem before suggesting the next step.

Required fields are marked in the form. The browser will check that the required fields are completed before the form can be submitted.

Before you send feedback

A useful backlog question usually explains the user, the problem, the expected value, and any constraint that affects the work.

  • Who is affected by the request?
  • What problem needs to be solved?
  • What outcome would make the work successful?
  • Are there deadlines, risks, or dependencies?
